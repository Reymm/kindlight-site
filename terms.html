What Kindlight is — and is not

Kindlight provides general caregiving education and communication coaching for family caregivers of adults with dementia. Its guidance is drawn from published sources including the Alzheimer's Association, the US National Institute on Aging, the Alzheimer's Society, and Teepa Snow's Positive Approach to Care, and is reviewed before publication.

Kindlight is not a medical service. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, stage, or treat any condition, does not assess any person's capacity, and does not provide medication advice of any kind. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always check with your person's doctor before making medical decisions. Kindlight does not monitor anyone and cannot summon help: in an emergency, call 911.

Safety first

Some situations should never be handled with coaching — sudden confusion or rapid change, a missing person, choking, injury, or danger to anyone. Kindlight's guidance routes those situations to emergency and same-day medical help, and you agree to treat those routes as the correct response rather than continuing to use coaching content.

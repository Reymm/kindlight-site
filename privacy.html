Privacy Policy

The short version: in this version of Kindlight there is no account, what you enter stays on your phone, and we never sell personal information. The long version is below, in plain language.

Last updated: November 2026.

What Kindlight stores, and where

Kindlight is designed to work on your device. The information you enter — your person's name, their dementia type and stage as you report them, the calming details you add, and your "what worked" notes — is stored on your phone, so the app can personalise its guidance for your household. In this version there is no Kindlight account and this information is not uploaded to our servers.

Voice

Kindlight can read guidance aloud using your phone's built-in text-to-speech. If a future feature processes voice input using a third-party service, the app will say so clearly and ask for your explicit permission before any audio or text is sent — and will not send it without that permission.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information. Ever.
  • We do not use your family's information for advertising.
  • We do not use one family's notes or "what worked" history to advise another family.
  • Crisis and safety information in the app works without an account and without sharing anything.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Those stores process your payment; we do not receive or store your card details. The stores share limited subscription status information (such as whether a subscription is active) so the app can unlock paid features.

This website

This website is a static site. It sets no advertising cookies. If we add privacy-respecting usage statistics later, we will update this policy first and describe what is collected in plain language here.

Email

If you email us at hello@getkindlight.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply and to improve Kindlight. Please don't send us medical details about your person — we don't need them to help you, and email isn't the right place for them.

Children

Kindlight is for adult family caregivers. It is not directed at children, and the care guidance in the app concerns adults with dementia.

Deleting your information

Because your information lives on your phone, deleting the app deletes it. If you have emailed us and would like those emails deleted, write to us and we will do so.

Changes

If we change how Kindlight handles information — for example, if a future version adds accounts or cloud backup — we will update this policy, note the change at the top, and, where the app can, tell you in the app before the change applies to you.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@getkindlight.com.

Privacy Terms Support Moment Guides

Kindlight provides general caregiving education and communication coaching. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always check with a doctor before making medical decisions. In an emergency, call 911.

Support

Two kinds of help live on this page: help with the app, and help for you. If the second kind is what you need right now, skip straight to the helplines — they're staffed by people who understand this exact life.

If anyone is in danger, injured, missing, or unwell, call 911 now (US & Canada) and say you care for a person with dementia.

If you might act on thoughts of harming yourself, call or text 988 in the US, or 9-8-8 in Canada — both are free, confidential, and answered 24/7.

Caregiver helplines — United States

  • Emergency: 911
  • Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline: 1-800-272-3900 — free, around the clock, staffed by master's-level clinicians; crisis assistance and local resources in 200+ languages.
  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988 · chat at 988lifeline.org
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741
  • Eldercare Locator: 1-800-677-1116 — local respite, Area Agencies on Aging, and Adult Protective Services routing (weekday hours).
  • Poison Help: 1-800-222-1222 — for suspected overdose or wrong-medication ingestion.
  • MedicAlert + Safe Return (wandering response): 1-800-625-3780
  • Medicare GUIDE program: many areas now offer a free dementia care navigator, a 24/7 support line, and respite through Medicare's GUIDE model. Ask your person's doctor's office whether they participate, or check eligibility — Kindlight can't enrol you, but a navigator is the single most useful human a caregiver can have.

Caregiver helplines — Canada

  • Emergency: 911
  • Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 9-8-8 — free, 24/7, bilingual.
  • Alzheimer Society of Canada — Information & Referrals: 1-855-705-4636 (Mon–Fri 9–5 ET, English/French). This is a referral line, not a crisis line — in crisis, call 911 or 9-8-8.
  • 211 Canada: dial 2-1-1 — free, confidential, 24/7 in most regions; local caregiver, respite, and seniors' services. Provincial First Link® dementia helplines also operate in several provinces — 211 can connect you.
  • Kids Help Phone (which also runs Canada's crisis text service): 1-800-668-6868, or text 686868.

Quick questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kindlight → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kindlight → Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and safety content stays free either way.

The app gave guidance that didn't fit my person. What now?

Trust your knowledge of your person over any script — always. Then, if you have a minute later, email us at hello@getkindlight.com and tell us the moment and what happened instead. The library is human-reviewed, and caregiver reports are how it gets better.

I found an error in a guide on this site.

Thank you — please email us with the page and the line. Accuracy matters more to us than being right the first time, and corrections are made with the source checked again.

Does Kindlight replace a care team or a dementia navigator?

No. Kindlight complements the humans — your person's doctor, and programs like Medicare GUIDE navigators or Alzheimer Society First Link staff. If you don't have a navigator yet, the helplines above are the fastest route to one.

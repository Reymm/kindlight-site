Built on reviewed guidance. Never improvised.

Kindlight's coaching comes from a fixed library of guidance drawn from the Alzheimer's Association, the US National Institute on Aging, the Alzheimer's Society, and Teepa Snow's Positive Approach to Care — reviewed by humans before it ever reaches you. In the moment, the app selects the right guidance and personalises it with your person's name and what has calmed them before. It does not invent advice on the spot.

Kindlight is not a medical service. It does not diagnose, stage, or treat dementia, and it never advises on medication. It is education and communication coaching for caregivers. Always check with your person's doctor before making medical decisions — and if a change is sudden, treat it as medical, not behavioural (see any guide above for the sudden-change warning).

If anyone is in danger or unwell, call 911 (US & Canada).

Questions families ask

Is Kindlight a medical service?

No. Kindlight provides general caregiving education and communication coaching based on published guidance from organisations like the Alzheimer's Association and the National Institute on Aging. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and never advises on medication. Please check with your person's doctor before making medical decisions.

Where do the scripts come from?

From a fixed, human-reviewed library built on Alzheimer's Association, National Institute on Aging, Alzheimer's Society, and Positive Approach to Care guidance. The app selects and personalises reviewed guidance — it never makes up new advice in the moment. Each guide on this site lists its sources.

Does it work for Lewy body, vascular, or frontotemporal dementia?

Yes — the guidance changes by type where the sources say it should. The clearest example: hallucinations in Lewy body dementia are often a normal, non-frightening part of the disease, and the app carries the medication-sensitivity warning every Lewy body caregiver should know. Read the Lewy body guide.

What if it's an emergency?

Call 911. Kindlight's safety checks come before any coaching: sudden confusion, a missing person, choking, injury, or danger routes you straight to emergency and same-day medical help, never to a script. Crisis numbers are always free in the app, with no paywall and no account.

Is my family's information private?

In this version, Kindlight works without an account and stores what you enter — your person's name, what calms them, your notes — on your phone, not on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for the plain-language version.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and can be cancelled in your phone's subscription settings in a few taps. The annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can use the full app through a few hard evenings before paying anything.
